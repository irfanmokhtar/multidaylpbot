# BTC Daily Research — Sentiment & Technical Analysis

A rolling log of daily Bitcoin ($BTC) research. Each run prepends a new dated reading at the top.
**Only the latest reading plus the previous 3 are kept (4 total); older readings are dropped.**

Each reading covers:
- **Sentiment** — overall market mood, with emphasis on X (Twitter) chatter, plus Fear & Greed where available.
- **Technical Analysis** — price, trend, key support/resistance, momentum (RSI/MACD/MAs), and a short bias call.
- **Elliott Wave** — current wave count/structure, degree, the active impulse or corrective pattern, key invalidation level, and the projected next move.
